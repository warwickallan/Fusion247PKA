## The two findings this starts from — already established, do not re-derive

- **(a) is a producer/consumer naming mismatch, not missing data.** `footer.mjs` takes its numerator from `context_window.used_tokens`. **That field is not in the sample** — the producer writes **`total_input_tokens`**. So `usedTokens` is `null`, the module falls back to the reported percentage, and renders `ctx 42%` **while `418,491` sits unread on disk.** **Test this before designing anything; the fix may be one field name.**
- **(b) the footer never grounds its own recommendation.** With no `--next`, it renders `next: UNSET`. **The model/effort advice is CALLER-SUPPLIED.** It is not invented — telemetry independently records Opus/high — **but the module did not compute it, so any claim that the footer "recommends" a model is false as built.** Warwick's requirement is that it **inspect the remaining work in the active phase** and recommend from that.
