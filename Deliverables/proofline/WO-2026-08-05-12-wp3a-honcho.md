**(a) Reliable current-frontier read at present and expected store size.** *Expected* is the operative word — E-C says the growth is in pages, not page-1 items. **A silent partial read must become impossible or loud; it may not stay silent.**

**(b) Correct `map_path` delivery.** E-D/E-E: stripping is latent, **and n=1 under the guard is itself the masked case**, so the live census proves less than it appears to. **E-H is the timing that matters: the next packet write is AT ROTATION**, which is exactly when the pointer is load-bearing.

**(c) No fail-open write-authority guard.** Two constraints, and they pull against each other — resolve them, do not pick one: **the guard must not silently do the wrong thing when it cannot establish authority, AND a packet must not be silently lost.** E-I is the root: `writeContinuity:596-612` runs a full `readLatest` inside the write path and falls back to an unconditional write in a `catch`.
