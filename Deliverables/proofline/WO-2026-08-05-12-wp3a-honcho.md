## The outcome

**A fresh Larry, anywhere, is oriented to the correct active map and the current frontier — reliably, and honestly when it cannot be.**

## Build from measurement, not from theory — §16.5 is executed evidence

Read §16.5 in full before planning. The three that most constrain the design:

- **E-B: the 2026-08-05 abort was a TRANSIENT.** Ten of ten page-1 reads succeeded, worst case 9.8% of the 9,000 ms budget. **"The store grew past the timeout" is NOT established.** Do not build a fix premised on it.
- **E-C: page 1 is ALREADY at the `size:100` cap** (`total:149, pages:2`). Further growth adds **pages**, and **later-page failures are swallowed rather than raised** — a page-2 failure yields `complete:false`, not an error. **That is the real "expected store size" risk in (a), and it is silent.** A frontier read that quietly loses pages is the exact failure class this phase exists to close.
- **E-F: the two defects partially mask each other.** When `readLatest` inside `writeContinuity` times out, the guard fails open and `map_path` is **kept**. **A test that exercises them one at a time will pass while both are broken.**

## The four outcomes, with what each must not become

**(a) Reliable current-frontier read at present and expected store size.** *Expected* is the operative word — E-C says the growth is in pages, not page-1 items. **A silent partial read must become impossible or loud; it may not stay silent.**

**(b) Correct `map_path` delivery.** E-D/E-E: stripping is latent, **and n=1 under the guard is itself the masked case**, so the live census proves less than it appears to. **E-H is the timing that matters: the next packet write is AT ROTATION**, which is exactly when the pointer is load-bearing.

**(c) No fail-open write-authority guard.** Two constraints, and they pull against each other — resolve them, do not pick one: **the guard must not silently do the wrong thing when it cannot establish authority, AND a packet must not be silently lost.** E-I is the root: `writeContinuity:596-612` runs a full `readLatest` inside the write path and falls back to an unconditional write in a `catch`.

**(d) Stale or degraded fallback identifies itself AND still orients.** E-J: the success path already renders age and an orientation line; the `:932-933` failure branch renders neither **despite having parsed `updated_at`**. **A degraded render that is honest but leaves the reader with nowhere to go has met half the requirement and failed the other half.**

## Evidence bar

- **`node --test` from the repo root or the module's directory. Assert `# tests` ≥ expected AND `# fail 0` — never the exit code**, which returns 0 on zero tests (map §2).
- **Mutation-test every control you add.** A control is not evidence until it has been made to fail: break it, prove the test goes red, restore it, prove byte-identical restoration.
- **E-F demands a combined case.** At least one test must exercise the slow-read and the guard **together**, because that combination is what hid both faults.
- **Report raw numbers for any latency measurement — every repetition, no mean, no best-of.** A single sample decides nothing; that is the instrument error the Phase 2 gate made.
