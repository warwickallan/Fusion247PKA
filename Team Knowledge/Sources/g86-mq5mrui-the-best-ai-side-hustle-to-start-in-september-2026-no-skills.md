## Key concepts & takeaways

- **Distribution is the meta-lever**: nearly every model in the list is described as easier, more scalable, and more monetizable *with* an existing audience, and hard or capped *without* one.
- **AI closes moats faster than it opens them** in commoditizable services (websites, basic clipping) — the source's advice is to avoid businesses where the AI tool itself is the entire value proposition, since customers will soon access the same tool directly.
- **Human judgment persists as the differentiator** even in heavily AI-augmented workflows (clip selection, newsletter copywriting) — full automation claims for these tasks are treated skeptically by someone who has actually run them at scale.
- **Businesses buy agency, not expertise** — the strongest single reframe in the video: what small-business consulting clients actually pay for is someone who will doggedly brute-force a solution with AI tools, not necessarily someone with prior domain mastery.
- **Ticket size should scale with proven infrastructure**, not be jumped to directly — the source presents an explicit progression (low-ticket → small business consulting → high-ticket consulting) rather than "start high."
- **Never make a volatile, skill-dependent income stream (trading) your sole income** — diversify the income base first, then layer trading on top once financial pressure is removed from the trading decision.
